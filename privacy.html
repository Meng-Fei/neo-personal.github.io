Privacy policy

neo-personal is used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google account (Calendar and Drive).

Data from Google APIs is stored only on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own Google Drive. It is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone else.

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
